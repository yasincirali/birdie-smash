Birdie Smash is a badminton game you play with one finger. When the shuttle comes, you swipe: the direction of your swipe decides where it goes, the speed decides how hard you hit.

- Career: 8 tours, 48 matches and six playing styles, from your local gym to the Legends Cup, then the endless Champion road
- Match of the day: a new opponent, court and twist every day, with no streaks to keep
- Endless rally: the coach feeds until you miss, faster every 5 hits
- Locker room: shirts, caps, shoes, celebrations and smash moves unlocked by level; racket, strings and grip that change how you play

SHOTS
Pass over the shuttle slowly and briefly for a drop that falls just behind the net. Swipe long for a clear to the back line. Swipe fast with the shuttle above your head and you smash. Drive at shoulder height, lift a low shuttle, kill it at the net, block the opponent's smash. Draw an arc as you swipe and you slice the shuttle so it lands shorter than expected. You serve too: short, flick or high.

Hits through the middle of the shuttle charge your smash power. When it is full, your next smash is a jump smash. String perfect hits together to reach Flow: the lines turn gold, the target grows and the music gains a harmony.

STAY FIT
Smashes, jumps and lunges burn stamina. When it runs low you slow down and your smash weakens. Play clears and rest between points. Forehand and backhand are different: there is no smash on the backhand side, so position yourself to take the shuttle on the forehand.

CAREER
Eight tours: the Local road, the Asia Tour, the Europe Tour, the World Finals, the Pacific Tour, the Americas Tour, the Grand Cup and the Legends Cup, six opponents each. Opponents play differently: the Smasher smashes every overhead, the Wall returns everything, the Net Player lives at the net, the Trickster changes direction at the last moment. Tour bosses have a signature shot you learn to read. Every match comes with missions and stars. After the World Finals the Champion road begins: an endless ladder played at the difficulty you choose. On Pro it is called the Master road and you do the running too.

GEAR
Win matches to earn Feathers and XP. Racket model, string tension, wristband, headband and shirt change how the game plays: a head-heavy racket hits harder smashes, a head-light racket speeds up the net game, loose strings give power, tight strings give control. Your gear also changes the racket sound and the haptics. Spin your player 360 degrees and inspect your racket up close. As you level up you unlock new moves for the jump smash; they are looks only and are never sold. After level 30 the Legend ranks begin. Feathers are earned by playing; if you want more, Feather packs are available as in-app purchases.

TRAINING AND QUICK MATCH
A coached training session teaches every shot step by step. In Quick match choose a 5, 11 or 21 point game on one of four courts: hall, tournament, wooden school gym and night. Four difficulty levels: in Easy and Medium your player runs to the shuttle and you just hit; in Hard and Pro you do the running too, and in Hard your player helps when you are late.

FEEL AND SOUND
The racket sound is generated on your phone from the string tension and the type of shot. Every hit comes with haptic feedback. With Reduce Motion on, transitions stay calm.

GAME CENTER
Game Center achievements for your career and in-game feats. Signing in is optional.

NO INTERNET NEEDED
The game runs on your phone with no account needed; your progress is stored on your device. This free game shows a small ad banner in the menus, and after a match you can choose to watch a short ad to boost your Feather reward. No ads interrupt a match. A one-time No Ads purchase removes the menu banner.

Available in English and Turkish, following your phone's language.
