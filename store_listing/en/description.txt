Birdie Smash is a badminton game you play with one finger. When the shuttle comes, you swipe: the direction of your swipe decides where it goes, the speed decides how hard you hit.

SHOTS
Pass over the shuttle slowly and briefly for a drop that falls just behind the net. Swipe long for a clear to the back line. Swipe fast with the shuttle above your head and you smash. Drive at shoulder height, lift a low shuttle, kill it at the net, block the opponent's smash. Draw an arc as you swipe and you slice the shuttle so it lands shorter than expected. You serve too: short, flick or high.

Hits through the middle of the shuttle charge your smash power. When it is full, your next smash is a jump smash. String perfect hits together to reach Flow: the lines turn gold, the target grows and the music gains a harmony.

STAY FIT
Smashes, jumps and lunges burn stamina. When it runs low you slow down and your smash weakens. Play clears and rest between points. Forehand and backhand are different: there is no smash on the backhand side, so position yourself to take the shuttle on the forehand.

CAREER
Six stages: local gym, school tournament, city championship, regional final, national league and the Champions Cup. Opponents play differently: the Smasher smashes every overhead, the Wall returns everything, the Net Player lives at the net. Every match comes with three missions. Finish the career to unlock the Champion shirt.

GEAR
Win matches to earn Feathers and XP. Racket model, string tension, wristband, headband and shirt change how the game plays: a head-heavy racket hits harder smashes, a head-light racket speeds up the net game, loose strings give power, tight strings give control. Your gear also changes the racket sound and the haptics. Spin your player 360 degrees and inspect your racket up close. Feathers are earned only by playing; there are no purchases.

TRAINING AND QUICK MATCH
A coached training session teaches every shot step by step. In Quick match choose a 5, 11 or 21 point game on one of four courts: hall, tournament, wooden school gym and night. In Easy mode your player runs to the shuttle and you just hit. In Pro mode you do the running too.

FEEL AND SOUND
The racket sound is generated on your phone from the string tension and the type of shot. Every hit comes with haptic feedback. With Reduce Motion on, transitions stay calm.

GAME CENTER
Game Center achievements for your career and in-game feats. Signing in is optional.

NO INTERNET NEEDED
The game runs entirely on your phone. No account, no ads, no data collected; your progress is stored only on your device.

Available in English and Turkish, following your phone's language.
