# Birdie Smash Privacy Policy

Version: 1 · Effective: 24 October 2026

Birdie Smash is a badminton game that runs entirely on your phone. This policy describes what the game actually does.

## In short

- The game **collects no personal data** and sends no data to us.
- The game does not connect to the internet. We have no servers.
- No account is needed. You are never asked for a name, e-mail address or phone number.
- There are no ads. There is no analytics, tracking or third-party tracking tool.
- There are no in-app purchases. "Feathers" in the game are earned only by playing.

## What stays on your device

Your progress is stored only on your phone, in the game's own local storage:

- settings (difficulty, match length, court, game mode),
- your gear, Feathers, level and upgrades,
- your career stage, records and unlocked achievements,
- whether you have finished the training.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud backup or a computer backup) may include app data. That backup is controlled by Apple and by you; it never reaches us.

## Game Center (optional)

Birdie Smash can use Apple's Game Center service for achievements. The game offers Game Center sign-in only after you win the first career stage; signing in is not required.

If you sign in, the game only reports the achievements you have unlocked to Game Center. This goes to Apple, not to us. Game Center is operated by Apple and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies. The game does not read your name, e-mail address or friends list from Game Center, and it does not store your player ID or send it anywhere else.

You can turn Game Center off at any time in iPhone Settings › Game Center; the game keeps working with your local progress.

## Sound, haptics and permissions

Racket sounds and music are generated on your phone. Haptics use your phone's vibration motor. The game does not ask for microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or notification permission.

## Information Apple collects

The App Store keeps download and sales statistics as Apple. If you have turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone settings, Apple may share crash reports and aggregated usage statistics with developers. Apple collects this information and handles it under its own policy; the game itself adds nothing to it. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, whatever their age. It therefore collects no data from children.

## Your rights

Because we keep no records about you, there is no data of yours for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove the progress on your device by deleting the game. For your Game Center data, contact Apple.

## Changes

If the game ever gains a feature that collects data, this policy will be updated first, its version number will go up and the App Store privacy label will change to match.

## Contact

You can send questions through the support link on the Birdie Smash page in the App Store.
